Parliamentary Procedure

Rules of Procedure (ROP)

NIRMAAN MUN 2026 — Create. Converse. Change.

1. The Roll Call

At the beginning of each session, the Chair will call the names of all member states. Delegates respond with "Present" or "Present and Voting".

2. The Speaker's List

The primary form of debate. Once the list is open, delegates may add their names to speak on the general topic. Speakers have a set time limit (usually 60-90 seconds).

3. Caucuses

Moderated Caucus:

A mixture of the Speaker's List and informal debate. The Chair calls on delegates to speak for a short time (e.g., 45 seconds) on a specific sub-topic.

Unmoderated Caucus:

A temporary suspension of the rules. Delegates can leave their seats to speak informally with others and draft resolutions.

4. Points & Motions

Point / Motion Purpose Interrupt?
Point of Personal Privilege Address discomfort (too hot, can't hear). Yes
Point of Order Correct a procedural error. No
Point of Inquiry Ask a question about the rules. No
Motion to Adjourn End the session for the day. No

5. Resolutions

Resolutions are the final documents of the committee. They require Sponsors (authors) and Signatories (those who wish to see it debated). Voting requires a simple majority to pass.
